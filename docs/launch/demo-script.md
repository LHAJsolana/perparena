# Demo Video Script

Target length: 60-90 seconds.

## 1. Opening Problem

"PerpArena is a simulated trading competition prototype. The problem it explores is simple: leaderboards should not reward only account size, excessive leverage, artificial volume, or one lucky trade."

## 2. Homepage

"The homepage frames the product thesis and shows the synthetic competition summary. The disclaimer is visible: no real trades, no custody, no financial advice, and no real rewards."

## 3. Leaderboard

"The competition page shows the public leaderboard for synthetic participants. Rankings are based on a transparent competition score, not raw volume alone."

## 4. Filters

"Filters let viewers search wallets, switch divisions, filter by market participation, filter by integrity status, sort metrics, and paginate without loading everything into the browser."

## 5. Trader Profile

"Opening a synthetic trader profile shows rank, division, score, simulated performance, risk metrics, and recent synthetic trades."

## 6. Score Breakdown

"The score breakdown explains each component: performance, risk management, consistency, qualified activity, and market diversity. Each component has caps and rationale."

## 7. Integrity Engine

"The Integrity Engine surfaces simulation-based signals such as excessive leverage or one-trade dependence. It does not claim conclusive fraud detection."

## 8. Admin Configuration

"The admin area is a demonstration control center. Production mutations are disabled by default, and the interface clearly labels itself as a demo environment."

## 9. Architecture

"Under the hood, PerpArena uses Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Zod, Vitest, Playwright, Recharts, and separated domain engines for analytics, scoring, integrity, and engagement."

## 10. Closing CTA

"PerpArena is a portfolio case study in risk analytics, simulation, and honest product design. Review the repository for the architecture, tests, methodology, and limitations."
